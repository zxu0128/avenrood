Zihan Avenrood Xu is a photographer and visual artist based in Canada, born in Ürümqi. Their work mainly captures coincidence, decay, subtext, and absurdity within urban and natural landscapes across North America and Central Asia.


avenrood's work can be seen on Apple, Kinfolk Magazine, Fisheye Magazine, Rental Magazine and more.
