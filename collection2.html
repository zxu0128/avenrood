Theatre of Leaves


This series is about the fleeting dramaturgy of fallen leaves, small performers caught between chance and inevitability. Drifted and carried without intention, these leaves come to rest against the varied geometries of the city: roads, nets, cars, machines, windows. Their fragile pauses echo the human condition, they speak to our beauty, vulnerability, and our quiet resilience in the face of life’s impermanence.
"Everything is Leaf" – Goethe.
